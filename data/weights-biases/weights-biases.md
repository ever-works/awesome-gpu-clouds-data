## Overview

Weights & Biases is an MLOps platform for experiment tracking, model registry, and performance monitoring for GPU training runs.

## Features

- Experiment tracking for ML training
- Model registry and versioning
- Performance monitoring and visualization
- GPU training run tracking
- Hyperparameter optimization
- Collaboration tools for ML teams

## Pricing

Paid. Free tier available for individuals; team and enterprise plans with usage-based pricing.
