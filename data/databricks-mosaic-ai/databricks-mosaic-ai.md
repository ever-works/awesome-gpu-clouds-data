## Overview

Databricks Mosaic AI is a unified platform for fine-tuning, training, and serving LLMs inside the Databricks Lakehouse.

## Features

- Fine-tuning, training, and serving LLMs
- Integrated with Databricks Lakehouse
- End-to-end MLOps for generative AI
- GPU-accelerated training workloads
- Unity Catalog for governance

## Pricing

Paid. Pricing based on Databricks DBUs and GPU instance usage.
